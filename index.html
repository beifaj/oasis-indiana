Why Oasis Indiana?

Because life is easier when you don't have to figure it out alone.

College. Faith. Relationships. Career decisions. Pressure. Purpose. Friendships. Adulthood. Sometimes you need more than another meeting, you need people you can actually do life with.

  • BelongBuild genuine friendships and find a community where you can be known, supported, and valued.
  • Real TalkHonest conversations about the things young people actually navigate: relationships, academics, purpose, pressure, career, identity, and everyday life.
  • GrowDiscover how God's Word applies beyond church and learn to live out your faith in practical, everyday situations.
  • ConnectMeet other students and young people, build meaningful relationships, network, serve, and grow together.
  • EnjoyGames, conversations, hangouts, workshops, outreach, celebrations, and moments that make community feel like community.
  • PrayNeed someone to stand with you? Prayer and spiritual support are part of who we are.

Oasis Compass · Virtual community · 18+

You don't need a campus to have an Oasis.

Oasis Compass is our virtual community for young adults navigating life's next steps: faith, purpose, education, career, relationships, decisions, and everything in between.

Maybe you're figuring out whether college is right for you. Maybe you've graduated and you're asking, “What now?” Or maybe you simply want a community that helps you grow with God while figuring life out.

Compass creates room for real conversations, mentorship, practical life and career sessions, faith, prayer, networking, and community, wherever you are in Indiana.

You don't have to have everything figured out to start moving in the right direction.

Join Oasis Compass

Why “Compass”?

Because seasons of transition come with questions: Where am I going? What should I do next? What is my purpose? How do I make the right decisions?

Our foundation is God's promise:

“I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye.” Psalm 32:8

Under 18? Compass is for ages 18 and over. If you're still in high school, email us at [[ OASIS EMAIL ]] and we'll connect you with a youth ministry near you. Parents are welcome to reach out first.

The bigger vision

From one campus to communities across Indiana.

Oasis Indiana began with a simple conviction: young people need places where they can belong, be heard, discover purpose, grow spiritually, and receive practical support for the life they are actually living.

Our vision is to raise communities across Indiana, on campuses and beyond them, where young people are empowered to know who they are, develop meaningful relationships, make better decisions, serve others, grow in their relationship with God, and become positive influences wherever they go.

Our story

It started with creating a place for students to belong.

Oasis Indiana grew from the work already being built through Oasis Haven Spot at Indiana University Indianapolis, a student community centered on real conversations, genuine relationships, faith, purpose, and meeting the practical needs students face.

Now the vision is growing beyond one campus: establishing communities across Indiana while also creating a virtual pathway for young people who may not currently have an Oasis where they are.

Oasis Indiana is part of the wider Oasis ministry family, connected through Oasis Midwest and Oasis USA, a LoveWorld campus ministry. You can find the national ministry at blwoasis.com.

Rhapsody of Realities, one of the devotional resources we distribute, is published by Believers' LoveWorld (Christ Embassy).
