What a night looks like

One hour. You'll know by the end.

Every Oasis runs the same rhythm. Walk in late, sit at the back, say nothing. Nobody will put you on the spot.

First 10

Just arrive

Food, music, no program. Someone will find you and it won't be weird.

10–25

Something fun together

Karaoke, trivia, finish the gospel lyric. Laughing first makes the rest possible.

25–45

The real conversation

Finances, exams, family, comparison, loneliness. What's said here stays here.

45–55

What the Word says

Tied to what we actually talked about, never bolted on. Ask anything.

Last 5

Pray, then go

We pray for what came up. Sitting that out is completely fine. Out by the hour.

Talk shows

Our story

It started with one room.

Oasis Haven Spot at IU Indianapolis: some food, and students talking about what college is actually like instead of how it looks online.

Listening there is where Launch came from. Underneath the exams and the pressure, the same sentence kept coming up: I don't really know what I'm doing.

If it's a heavy season

You don't have to carry it on your own.

This is the whole reason Oasis exists. Not a service. People who notice when you've gone quiet, text you back, and pray with you if that's what you want.

Tell one of us. You won't be a burden and you won't be handled. You'll just have company.

And if it's more than a conversation can hold, we'll help you reach someone trained, whether that's campus counseling or the 988 line, and stay with you while you do it.

There's a place for you here.
