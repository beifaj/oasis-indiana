One hour, every week

Here’s exactly what happens.

No surprises, no being put on the spot, no performance. You can arrive late, sit at the back, say nothing at all, and eat the food. Nobody will mind.

0–10

Arrive

Food out, music on, no programme. If it’s your first time, someone will find you, and it will not be awkward.

10–15

Read together

We read the day’s devotional out loud. Short, and somebody different reads it each week.

15–45

Real Talk

One topic students are actually living through. Heartbreak, money, failure, loneliness, friendship, purpose. Real situations come up on screen and the room talks about them.

45–55

What the Word says

Scripture applied to the thing we just discussed, not bolted on afterwards. You can ask anything, including the difficult questions.

55–60

Pray and go

We pray for what came up. You are welcome to be prayed for, and equally welcome to sit that out. Out on time.

Also every week

Fifteen minutes, Friday nights.

We pray together online every Friday. Fifteen minutes, nothing more, and it is open to absolutely everyone. You do not have to be a student, you do not have to have been to Real Talk, and you do not have to say anything.

Fridays 9:30 – 9:45 PM ET

Come with your camera off if that is easier. Bring something you want prayed for, or bring nothing and just listen.

Who we are

Christian, and upfront about it.

Oasis Indiana is a Christian student community. We read Scripture, we pray together, and Jesus is the centre of why we do this. We would rather tell you that now than have you find out later.

You are welcome here whatever you believe, and nobody is going to corner you, chase you, or make you feel strange for asking hard questions. Plenty of people come for the conversation and the people, and that is a completely fine reason to come.

We are part of Oasis Midwest, the campus ministry of Believers’ LoveWorld.
