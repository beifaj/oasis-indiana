What actually happens

One hour. You'll know if it's for you by the end of it.

Every Oasis runs on the same rhythm, wherever it is. You can walk in late, sit at the back, say nothing, and eat the food. Nobody will put you on the spot.

First 10

Just arrive

Food out, music on, no program. Nobody has to walk into a circle cold. If it's your first time, someone will find you, and it won't be weird.

10 – 25

Something fun together

Karaoke, Bible trivia, finish the gospel lyric, a team challenge where nobody is the expert. Laughing together is what makes the next part possible, and it's the part people tell their friends about.

25 – 45

The real conversation

Relationships, family, money, exam pressure, comparison, loneliness, what you're actually afraid of this semester. Students talk first. What's said here stays here, and nobody gets fixed on the spot.

45 – 55

What the Word says about it

Bible study, tied to whatever we just talked about rather than bolted on. Short, honest, and you can ask anything, including the awkward questions.

Last 5

Prayer, then go

We pray for whatever came up. You're welcome to be prayed for, and equally welcome to sit that out. Then it's announcements and you're gone by the hour.

Once a month we do something bigger: a prayer walk across campus, a movie night, a games tournament, or a survival night in exam season.

Oasis Indiana Launch · Open to everyone · Always free

Everything students actually deal with, and nobody actually covers.

Launch is our open program. Free events, workshops and mentoring for any young person in Indiana, whether you're in college, heading there, taking a different route, or still working out what you want. There is no faith requirement and nothing here asks you to believe anything.

Direction

Working out what you want

Launch Day is one Saturday where you figure out what you're actually good at, look at every path after high school side by side, and leave holding a written plan with real next steps and real costs. For anyone unsure about a major, a career, or whether college is even the route.

Getting through

Exam therapy and the hard weeks

Sound therapy sessions in exam season: mats on the floor, eye masks, music, lights down, an hour where nobody needs anything from you. Study lock-ins with food and company. Movie nights when the week has been long. Practical, not preachy.

Money

The part nobody explains

Financial aid you might not know you qualify for. FAFSA. 21st Century Scholars. Budgeting when you're broke. The difference between debt that buys you something and debt that doesn't.

Real talk

Talk shows and honest conversation

Live student talk shows on the things people actually discuss at 1am: relationships, dating, peer pressure, family stuff, loneliness, comparison, identity. Student hosts, real guests, audience questions.

Skills and fun

Workshops, games and making things

Bead making, knitting, cooking, CV and interview clinics, creative nights, socials, games. Sometimes the point is a new skill. Sometimes the point is just being in a room with people and not thinking about your GPA.

Your corner

Someone who has been where you are

Everyone who does a Launch Day gets someone checking in on how it's going. Not strangers who signed up online. People we've trained ourselves from inside our own communities, most of them students who were sitting where you are a year or two ago and know exactly how it felt.

Straight up, before you sign up

Oasis Indiana is a Christian student organization. Launch isn't a church service and it isn't a recruiting exercise. It's about your life as a student, and you'll never be asked what you believe to take part.

When you register there are two optional boxes: one to hear about future Launch events, and one to hear about the Oasis community on your campus. Tick either, both, or neither. We keep the two completely separate.

Why we exist

Indiana is great at graduating students. Then a lot of them stop.

This isn't about anyone failing. It's about a step nobody walks you through, and a set of pressures nobody warns you about.

91.8% Finish high school in Indiana

Class of 2025. The highest rate the state has ever recorded.

52.2% Go on to anything after it

Class of 2024. College, trade school, a credential, an apprenticeship. Ten years ago it was around 65%.

We're good at getting people across the stage. We're not good at what happens the morning after.

If any of this is you

You're not behind. Nobody explained this part to anyone.

These are the things students tell us most. If you recognize yourself in one of them, that's the whole reason we're here.

  • No planEveryone keeps asking what's next and you're tired of pretending you have an answer.
  • Wrong pathYou picked a major because it sounded right, or because someone else wanted it, and now you're not sure it's yours.
  • ExamsMidterms hit and everything else falls apart. You know you should have started earlier and that doesn't help you now.
  • MoneyAround 59% of college students nationally have thought about leaving because of financial stress, and only about one in five felt they understood their own aid offer. The confusion costs people as much as the cost does.
  • Heavy weeksIn 2024, about 30% of Indiana students in grades 7 to 12 said they'd felt persistently sad or hopeless. That's down from 36% in 2022, and it still means you are nowhere near alone.
  • PressureRelationships, family expectations, comparison, the friend group that pulls you somewhere you don't want to go. Everyone else looks like they're handling it. Most of them aren't.
  • AloneYou moved, or you commute, or your people all went somewhere else. Making friends after eighteen is genuinely harder and nobody warns you.

Sources: Indiana Youth Institute, 2026 Indiana KIDS COUNT Data Book. Indiana Commission for Higher Education college-going data. Ellucian and EMI Research Solutions national student survey, 2024.

How this works

Two ways in, and you choose which.

Oasis Indiana is a Christian student organization. We say that upfront because we'd rather you know exactly who we are than find out later. Here's what it does and doesn't mean.

Oasis Indiana Launch

Our free events, workshops and mentoring, covering the whole of student life from exams to money to what you do next.

No faith requirement of any kind. You'll never be asked what you believe, and taking part commits you to nothing else.

Our Oasis communities

Openly Christ-centered

Weekly student groups on campus, plus Oasis Compass online. Honest conversation about real student challenges, with Scripture as the anchor.

These are openly Christian and open to anyone curious. You can do Launch and never come to one. That's completely fine, and it changes nothing about how we treat you.

Oasis Compass · Thursdays 6–7 PM ET · On Google Meet

Not at IU Indianapolis or Butler? This is your Oasis.

Compass is our online Christ-centered community, and it's the front door for every student who doesn't have an Oasis group on their campus yet.

Maybe your school doesn't have one. Maybe you're still in high school. Maybe you're moving to Indiana and want people before you arrive. Maybe you've graduated and you're asking what now. You're all in the right place.

It's the same thing our campus groups do, online: honest conversation about relationships, exams, pressure, money, purpose and everything else, with Scripture as the anchor. Plus prayer, and people who actually notice when you go quiet.

You don't need to bring anything or be anything. Show up, listen, say as much or as little as you want. Plenty of people sit quiet for the first few weeks and that's completely normal.

You don't have to have it figured out to belong somewhere.

We meet Thursdays, 6–7 PM Eastern, on Google Meet. Same link every week. Come with your camera off if that's easier, nobody minds.

Why “Compass”?

Because the in-between seasons come with questions. Where am I going? What do I do next? How do I make a decision I won't regret?

Our foundation is God's promise:

“I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye.” Psalm 32:8

Wish there was one on your campus? Join Compass first and get to know us. If it's still on your mind later, email oasis@loveworldmidwest.org and we'll take it from there together. Nobody does that part alone.

Under 18? High schoolers are welcome, with a parent or guardian's consent. All sessions are group sessions with more than one adult present, and we don't message under-18s privately. Parents, email us first at oasis@loveworldmidwest.org and we'll walk you through it.

If it's a heavy season

You don't have to carry it on your own.

This is the whole reason Oasis exists. Not a service or a program. People who notice when you've gone quiet, text you back, sit with you, and pray with you if that's what you want.

If something heavy is going on, tell one of us. You won't be a burden and you won't be handled. You'll just have company.

And if it's more than a conversation can hold, we'll help you get to someone trained, whether that's your campus counseling center or the 988 line, and we'll stay with you while you do it. You never have to make that call by yourself.

Our story

It started with making one place where students could be honest.

Oasis Indiana grew out of Oasis Haven Spot at Indiana University Indianapolis: a room, some food, and students talking about what student life is actually like instead of how it looks online.

Listening there is where Launch came from. Underneath the exams and the money and the loneliness, the same sentence kept coming up: I don't really know what I'm doing. So we built something that answers it, and opened it to everyone.

Now there's a second community at Butler, and the goal is an Oasis within reach of every student in this state.

Oasis Indiana is part of Oasis Midwest, under Oasis USA, the campus expression of LoveWorld Inc., founded by Pastor Chris Oyakhilome. Oasis is on campuses across multiple states, and you can find them all at blwoasis.com.

Two things we point people to beyond our meetings: Rhapsody of Realities, the daily devotional we hand out free, and Foundation School, for anyone who wants to properly understand what they believe rather than just absorb it. Both published by Believers' LoveWorld (Christ Embassy).
